A rhetorical or venting question that asks for no new action is NONE.
“Not possible unless X” or “only if X” states a prerequisite without a promise, so it’s REFUSAL. CONDITIONAL needs a real commitment that kicks in once the condition is met.
Condition words (“unless”, “if”) don’t decide the label, just as “promise” doesn’t.
A joke or sarcastic statement still gets its own NONE span.
Each statement with a different label gets its own span, matching the prompt’s rule 2.
